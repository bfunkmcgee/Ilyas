# Act I, the Pit: beats 1 and 2 (copy of the user's doc tab, rev 8)

From the night of the fall to the first morning Ilyas can hold Tamm's door, about six weeks pass. The sequence takes him from
helpless to dangerous again on one arm, and teaches the player movement, navigation, buying, inventory, and the style system along
the way.

## Beat 1: The Sump
### 1. Off the roof
*The Sump's roof, minutes after the fall.* The hay on the Sump's roof is bedding for Dob's pigeons, which he sells to Tamm's
kitchen. Dob is up there when an elf comes down through the birds. He runs for Tamm, and the two of them haul Ilyas through the roof
hatch and down to the kitchen table.

The shoulder is pumping blood. Tamm puts a skillet on the stove and does what has to be done. Ilyas surfaces once, long enough to see
a thread of gold light high on the Pit wall, where his arm hangs on the spike. Dob sees it too.

**Play:** a short cutscene with no input.
> **Dob:** "Tamm! An elf came down in the birds' hay!"
> **Tamm:** "Then get him out of the birds' hay."
> **Tamm:** "Hold his legs, Dob. And don't look."
> **Ilyas:** "My arm."
> **Tamm:** "Whatever's up that wall stays up that wall."

## Beat 2: A man's pace
### 2. The back room
*The Sump, three weeks later.* Ilyas wakes on a cot in a storeroom that smells of beer and onions. By habit he reaches for the gilt,
and nothing answers. His suit lies cut to ribbons on a chair, and the arc-blade's hilt sits on a shelf among the jars. Tamm comes in
with soup. She asks nothing about who he is, and he offers nothing.

**Play:** the first control the player has. Look around and interact: the window onto the Pit's floor, the soup, the ruined suit, the
hilt. Inspecting the hilt spills nine loose plates into his lap. The Aether tab flickers once in the command dock and is gone.
> **Tamm:** "Three weeks. You talked the whole time."
> **Ilyas:** "What did I say?"
> **Tamm:** "Names, mostly. Then you begged somebody for water."
> **Ilyas:** "Where is this?"
> **Tamm:** "The Sump. Bottom of the Pit. Nobody comes down here looking for anybody."
> **Ilyas** (inspecting the hilt): "Nine plates. Nothing to lock them."

### 3. Lost property
*The back room.* His clothes are past saving. Tamm drags out a crate of everything her customers have left behind over thirty years.
Nothing is cut for an elf. He settles on a collarless work shirt, an orc dockhand's oilcloth coat two sizes too big, a scarf, and
boots that nearly fit. Tamm pins the empty right sleeve up with a pin from her apron. In the cracked mirror over the washbasin there
is no gold on him anywhere.

**Play:** the equipment screen. Gear slots for body, coat, hands, feet and belt, with a few outfits to choose from the crate; the
coat adds a little knife resistance. The right arm slot shows a hatched outline labeled Missing, which sets up the scrap arm and the
joining. The look changes with it: Act I is soot, oilcloth and rust, and the Noir Gilt gold comes back only when the arm does.
> **Tamm:** "Thirty years of drunks leave things behind. Take what fits."
> **Ilyas:** "None of it fits."
> **Tamm:** "Then take what's warm."
> **Ilyas** (thought, at the mirror): "No gold. Not one line."

### 4. A man's pace
*The Sump's yard and back stair.* Tamm sends him to the yard pump and back. One arm has moved his center of weight, and he lurches
before he finds it. When he tries to run the way an elf runs, his legs give him a jog. Dob sits on the yard wall and supervises. At
the ladder to the roof loft, Ilyas reaches with a hand he does not have.

**Play:** the movement tutorial. Walk, run, turn and step between depth lanes on the 2.5D floor. Fill a bucket at the pump: his one
hand holds the bucket or nothing, so opening the gate means setting it down first. That is the hand slot. Ladders show a prompt,
Needs two hands, and the map marks them as closed routes until the scrap arm. His run is a man's run; the elf sprint stays locked
behind the gilt.
> **Dob:** "You walk like a drunk on payday."
> **Ilyas:** "I'm still finding where the floor is."
> **Dob** (at the ladder): "Stairs are round the side. Takes longer. Everything does now, I bet."

### 5. Tamm's list
*The stair-streets and the Shelf market.* Tamm hands him a canvas satchel, a short list, a purse of Crown coin and three pump
chits. The chits are Osgood Tarr's water paper, and in the Pit they pass for money.

The Pit has four tiers: the Floor where the Sump stands, the stair-streets switchbacking up the walls, the Shelf market on a broad
ledge halfway up, and the Rim under the tower's foot. From every stair the spike is in view. He stops at the first landing. The spike
is bare except for a dark stain and a strip of black coat cloth. Somebody climbed for the arm.

At the Shelf he works through the list. The pump valve comes from a stall run by a goblin, Nim, who looks at his tattoos longer than
at his coin. On the way back, the street goes quiet along a row of silent beggars with tin cups. Tamm told him never to drop a coin
in one. The old man at the end of the row turns his head and follows Ilyas with his eyes until he is out of sight.

**Play:** three tutorials in one errand.
- **Navigation.** The Pit map: its tiers, routes and landmarks, a waypoint for each item on the list, and the closed ladders that
  force the long way round.
- **Buying.** The shop screen, with prices in coin or chits. The good lamp oil costs a chit; the cheap oil smokes. An optional
  salve heals between fights, and a whetstone sharpens the dagger.
- **Inventory.** The satchel is a grid with slots and a weight limit. The rice sack takes three slots in the bag, or it can ride in
  his hand, which leaves no hand for a knife. Whatever is in his hand on the way home is what he has when the toll men step out.
> **Tamm:** "Lamp oil, rice, salt. A valve from the goblin at the pump stall. Clean linen for that shoulder."
> **Tamm:** "Chits are the Pump King's paper. Spend them before they're worth less."
> **Ilyas** (thought, at the spike): "Gone. Somebody climbed for it."
> **Nim:** "Ranger gilt."
> **Ilyas:** "You know it?"
> **Nim:** "I know what it guarded. Valve's two coin. For you, three."

### 6. The toll
*A stair-street below the Shelf, at dusk.* Two of Tarr's collectors close a switchback, one above him and one below. Thirty years of
habit answer for him, and he refuses. He reaches for the fight he knows, and most of it is not there.

The dagger is in his left hand. They act twice for every once he does. When he steps in, the bigger one gets behind him and pins his
only arm while the other goes to work. Twist free and the over-shoulder throw both need two hands. He goes down on the steps,
Broken, with Tamm's rice across the stair. Pinned and half out, he hears the smaller one say "Same ink as the arm in the pump..."
before the other cuts him off.

**Play:** the first fight of Act I, and one the player is meant to lose. The command dock shows the gaps: no Aether tab, the
arc-blade's techniques listed but dark, and a balance bar of four pips, a crew man's. He can land blows and should feel nearly
competent, never helpless. The scene ends when he is Broken a second time or falls under a third of his health. A skilled player who
downs one collector sees the other close a gang-up anyway, and earns a line from Tamm later. There is no game-over screen. The
cut-off line is the first clue toward beat 4.
> **Collector:** "Water toll on goods, friend. A chit a bag."
> **Ilyas:** "I've never paid a toll in my life."
> **Collector:** "Then you've been lucky. Pump King says everyone pays."
> **Collector** (over him): "Same ink as the arm in the pump..."
> **Collector:** "Shut it."

### 7. Tamm
*The same stair.* Tamm comes down because he is late and the oil is hers. She has a boning knife in her right hand, keeps her left
hand in her apron pocket the whole fight, and does not hurry.

The holder lets go of Ilyas to grab for her, and she cuts the reaching wrist. The other swings; she turns her empty side into it,
takes it on the shoulder, steps inside, and her knife is under his chin. She lets them both go with a message for Tarr, then picks
up the rice.

**Play:** a scene played by the game's own combat engine, with Tamm as a scripted fighter. It shows three of the moves the player
learns in scene 9: the Wrist toll, the Hinge and the Short ladder. Her closing line changes with the player's inventory choice in
scene 5.
> **Tamm:** "You're late. That oil is mine."
> **Collector:** "This is Pump King business, Tamm."
> **Tamm:** "The Sump pays its water on the first, same as always. Tell Osgood."
> **Tamm** (rice in the bag): "Well. The rice made it."
> **Tamm** (rice in his hand): "You dropped my rice."
> **Tamm** (if he downed one): "You put one down. With one hand and no light. Huh."

### 8. The yard at night
*The Sump's yard, that night.* He cannot sleep. In the yard he flicks the hilt, and nine plates rattle out into the dirt. He looks
up at the roof and calls the Glimmer step, and nothing comes. He picks the plates up one at a time with his only hand. Tamm comes
out with two mugs of tea and sits on the step.

**Play:** no fail state. The player can try the old inputs, unroll the blade and the Glimmer step, and each gives a short failed
animation and a thought. Then Tamm talks.
> **Ilyas** (thought): "I've cut down elves who could break her in half."
> **Ilyas** (thought): "Two of a slum lord's errand boys."
> **Ilyas** (thought): "Was any of it me? Or only the light?"
> **Tamm:** "You're thinking about the two on the stair."
> **Ilyas:** "I've gone through better than them by the dozen."
> **Tamm:** "With two arms and a lamp in your skin. Half the Pit saw that arm glowing on the wall."
> **Ilyas:** "Then you know what I was."
> **Tamm:** "I know what you are. One-handed and slow. Same as my grandfather when he came out of the Poisoned River."
> **Tamm:** "He ran a fish stall down here for forty years. Nobody robbed him twice."
> **Ilyas:** "Teach me."
> **Tamm:** "Sun-up. Bring the knife."

### 9. Short Hand
*The Sump's yard, a week of mornings.* The Tarkesh veterans called it Short Hand: short a hand, and short with the knife. They were
Caliphate soldiers who lost hands and arms in the Poisoned River war and came south in the dispersals with no trade but fighting.
They built a style out of what they had left, and Tamm learned it on the back step of her grandfather's fish stall.

Short Hand has to read clearly in the animation. The stance is low with the weight forward. The knife hand stays tight to the chest
and never reaches. The empty side turns slightly toward the enemy's weapon, and the steps are short and chopping, the feet never
crossing.

Each lesson is a drill, then a short bout against Tamm with a rolling pin.
1. **Stance.** Tamm shoves him round the yard with a broom while he holds his balance pips. Short Hand becomes his active style.
2. **The Hinge.** An answer that replaces the Parry while in Short Hand. He turns his empty side into the blow, takes it glancing on
   the shoulder, and ends inside the attacker's reach, where the dagger gets its +2.
3. **The Wrist toll.** An answer to a Grab or a Thrust: he cuts the reaching hand. A grab that meets it fails and costs the grabber
   two pips. It is the answer to what beat him on the stair.
4. **The Short ladder.** A strike: a rising stab from the low stance, under the guard. It hits hardest from inside reach.
5. **The Blind-side check and the Ankle hook.** The empty shoulder drives into a man as a cheaper Shoulder check, and the foot hooks
   behind his ankle while the knife threatens high. The yard's barrels and crates work as room moves.

The week ends with a bout to three touches. Tamm fights with her left hand in her apron pocket. When he takes the third touch, she
gives him the door.

**The styles screen** opens after the first lesson. Each weapon has several styles, each with its own origin and its own expertise.
Ilyas's three show what he has, what he lost, and what comes back:
| Style | Weapon | Origin | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short Hand | Dagger, one hand | Tarkesh veterans of the Poisoned River war, A.V. 1950 to 1983 | Novice, learning |
| Wire drill | Arc-blade | Noon Company, on the Confederacy's border wire, the 2270s | Dark: needs the gilt |
| The Sareth cut | Arc-blade, sword | Thirty years of Eclipsera enforcement; the clipped style the prototype already has | Locked: needs two hands |
> **Tamm:** "Empty side back. Knife close. Your feet are your other hand now."
> **Tamm:** "Don't block. You've nothing to block with. Take it on the shoulder and walk in."
> **Tamm:** "Anything that reaches for you pays the toll."
> **Tamm:** "Up from under. A man guards his face and forgets his belly."
> **Tamm** (after the third touch): "Door's yours, Friday nights. Don't kill anyone in my bar."
> **Ilyas:** "And outside it?"
> **Tamm:** "Outside it, don't let me hear about it."

## Design notes
The dark lattice is written into Ilyas's numbers, so the player feels the loss in every fight and every route, not only in cutscenes.
| | Prologue | Act I, dark lattice | After the joining |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balance | 6 pips | 4 pips, a crew man's | 6 pips |
| Speed | An elf's | A man's: every action costs more tempo | An elf's |
| Aether tab | Gilt bolt, Gale, Glimmer step | Gone | Back |
| Weapons | Arc-blade or any weapon | Hip dagger in the left hand; one-handed weapons only | All |
| Clinch | Full menu | No throw, no twist free | Full menu |
| Getting around | Sprint, ladders, Glimmer step | Jog; no ladders | All |
- **The losing fight** ends on a condition, not a game-over: Broken twice, or under a third of his health. A player who fights well
  still loses, but the game notices, through Tamm's line in scene 7.
- **Choices that get noticed.** Tamm's lines react to where the rice went and how the fight went.
- **Gates for later.** Closed ladders, two-handed clinch moves and two-handed weapons are all visible and locked in beats 1 and 2.
- **Seeds for beat 4.** The empty spike in scene 5, the collector's cut-off line in scene 6, and Tamm's "half the Pit saw that arm" in
  scene 8 all point at Tarr's pumphouse before anyone says it outright.
- **Open question.** Should the Sareth cut work, badly, with the scrap arm, or stay locked until the joining?
